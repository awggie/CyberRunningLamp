Bring-up order

  1. Measure the strings first. Put each string on the Jesverty in CC mode at 190 mA and note its forward voltage. Set the rail to amber Vf + 3.5 V, which should land near 28–30 V.
  2. Set the XL6019 with nothing on its output. Feed it 5 V and turn the trim pot until it reads the rail voltage from step 1. These modules can ship set to 35 V.
  3. Check the grounds. Confirm XL6019 IN− and OUT− are continuous.
  4. Test one channel. Wire J1 and J3 only, then jumper J2 pin 1 to 3.3 V in place of the XIAO. Measure about 189 mA in series with the LED wire.
  5. Check temperatures after 10 minutes. The LM317s, R3 pairs and R6 should be warm, not too hot to touch.
  6. Add the XIAO and the button, then flash the firmware.

Power limit. Both strings at 100% would draw about 2.8 A from USB, right at the 5 V / 3 A limit. The firmware caps their combined PWM at 1.25 strings' worth (about 2 A). Only arcing mode lights both at once, and there the amber dims slightly during each white flash. Use a USB-C charger rated 5 V / 3 A.
